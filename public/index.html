    HOW TO PLAY

    🥽 Meta Quest (VR)

    • Move like a gorilla: put your hands on the ground and push. Swing your arms back to run, push down hard to jump.
    • Climb: slap walls and pull yourself up. Push off walls to wall-jump onto rooftops.
    • Grip (either hand): grab a weapon or pickup near your hand.
    • Right trigger: shoot. A: reload. B: switch weapon.
    • X: jump boost. Y: menu. Left stick click: scoreboard.
    • Right stick: turn. Left stick: walk (if enabled in Settings).

    🖱 Desktop

    • WASD move, Shift sprint, Mouse look
    • Left click shoot, R reload, E pick up
    • Space jump (hold it against a ledge to climb up)
    • 1 2 3 / wheel / Q switch weapon
    • Tab scoreboard, Esc pause menu
    • Walk into a ladder to climb it.

    👮 Cop or 🧑 Civilian

    • Civilians can rob stores and steal cars. Crimes make you wanted and the police come for you: in a police car with the siren on, or straight out of the donut shop.
    • Cops spawn at the police station and can drive the police cars (L or X in VR: siren). Stores can't be robbed while a cop is close by.
    • Walk up to a wanted player and press E (or grip them in VR) to arrest them for a double bounty. Shooting innocent people costs cops $100.

    🏆 Rules

    • Everyone has 100 health. Headshots hurt a lot more.
    • Eliminated players respawn after 5 seconds.
    • Pistols hide in shops, shotguns at the police station, snipers on rooftops, rockets in red crates.
